# Industries route map

Route groups and child counts are listed in the approved `CANONICAL-ROUTES.txt` and derived `CANONICAL-SITEMAP.txt`. The route map contains 116 paths: `/`, 15 one-segment sector hubs, and 100 two-segment industry guides. Full titles, parent relationships, canonical URLs, and page types are in `data/industries/routes.manifest.json`.

To render a reviewed list by sector, inspect the catalog's `sectors[].guideSlugs` and the matching `industries[]` records. The compact top-level navigation only exposes the 15 hubs; deep guides remain available from search, hub cards, related routes, and contextual editorial links.
